# Community conduct · 社区行为准则

Treat contributors and users with respect. Keep feedback specific to the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unsolicited contact and publishing private data are unacceptable.

请尊重贡献者与使用者，围绕工作本身提出具体反馈。不接受骚扰、歧视、威胁、人肉搜索、未经请求的私下联系或公开他人隐私。

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community. Apply these decisions consistently and explain them privately where practical. Report conduct concerns through a private repository reporting channel when available; otherwise request a private contact without posting personal information.

维护者可移除有害内容、限制参与，并尽可能一致、清晰地解释处理决定。行为问题请通过私密渠道反馈；暂无渠道时，可请求私密联系，不在公开 issue 中披露个人信息。
